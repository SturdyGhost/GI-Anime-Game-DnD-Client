Through Mists of Smoke and Forests Dark

Archon Quest Chapter III, Act I — The party emerges into Sumeru's rainforest, chasing a vision of a white tree beneath a red sky.

Runtime: ~4 hours | Scenes: 9 | Combats: 4 | Puzzles: 1 | Player Choices: 4

Source: https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Through_Mists_of_Smoke_and_Forests_Dark

Load-bearing beats (do not cut): (1) The Irminsul vision in Haypasia's cave; (2) the Withering Zone cleanse; (3) the confrontation at Port Ormos where Mizri uses the Knowledge Capsule.

Prep Checklist (Before the Session)

  • ☐ Print this guide (double-sided, stapled)
  • ☐ Print player handouts — cut each along the dashed border, keep hidden until cue
  • ☐ Set up enemies in DM Hub Battle Prep: nature_samachurl, hillichurl_fighter, floating_nature_fungus, wooden_shield_mitachurl, grounded_earth_shroom, eremite_linebreaker, eremite_sword_dancer, eremite_ravenbeak_halberdier, electric_abyss_mage
  • ☐ Prepare the Irminsul Vision puzzle (see Scene 4): 6 small index cards with the symbols listed, plus the sealed "Whispered Memory" envelope
  • ☐ Bring a small incense stick or scented candle for the cave moment (optional atmosphere)
  • ☐ Have a purple token/bead on hand to represent the Padisarah flower if players pursue it
  • ☐ Review Scene 4 (Haypasia's cave) and Scene 8 (Port Ormos brawl) — they carry the session
  • ☐ Queue ambient rainforest audio for Scenes 1-4, bazaar/market audio for Scenes 6-8

Cast

Paimon — constant companion

Voice: High, quick, squeaky. Role: Exposition engine; voice any question the players forget to ask. Personality: Hungry, nosy, loud. Use her to summarize when the players look lost.

Tighnari — Forest Ranger, fox-eared scholar

Voice: Clipped, precise, faintly condescending when lectured to. Soft when he thinks no one's looking. Role: Gatekeeper of forest knowledge. He rations information based on trust. Personality: Cautious, protective of Collei, allergic to Akademiya politics.

Collei — Tighnari's apprentice

Voice: Quiet, careful, often trailing off. Warms up when she talks about Amber. Role: Emotional core of the forest arc. Personality: Touch-averse due to past trauma with Dottore; earnest, kind, ashamed of her illness (Eleazar).

Haypasia — wandering Akademiya researcher

Voice: Dreamy, distant, as if listening to something behind her own words. Role: The vision conduit. She is the reason the quest happens. Personality: Sleep-deprived, reverent, single-minded about Irminsul.

Dunyarzad — devotee of Lesser Lord Kusanali

Voice: Gentle, poised, a little formal. Coughs between sentences. Role: Introduces the Sabzeruz Festival subplot and the path to the Archon. Personality: Sickly but stubborn; grateful to the Archon for surviving childhood illness.

Dehya — Eremite mercenary, Dunyarzad's bodyguard

Voice: Low, lazy drawl, grinning through half her sentences. Role: The muscle; represents the Eremite perspective. Personality: Tough, fair, soft for kids and underdogs, allergic to pretension.

Alhaitham — Akademiya Scribe

Voice: Flat, deliberate, zero social warmth. Uses fewer words than a normal person would. Role: Reluctant ally with his own agenda. Personality: Brutally practical; does not perform politeness.

Mizri — Ayn Al-Ahmar zealot

Voice: Theatrical, rising to fevered proclamations. Shifts to a rasping echo after using the Capsule. Role: Scene 8 antagonist; living proof the Capsule destroys minds. Personality: True believer in King Deshret's return.

Nilou — dancer, follower of Lesser Lord Kusanali

Voice: Lilting, musical, often pauses mid-sentence to choose a prettier word. Role: Optional RP colour; asks for Padisarah flowers. Personality: Dreamy, sincere, a touch childlike.

Player Handouts

Cut each along the border before the session. The cue column is when to hand them over.

Handout 1 — Collei's Letter from Amber (cue: end of Scene 2)

Collei,
You'd never believe who wandered into Mondstadt. A traveler and their little floating friend — the same ones from the Stormterror incident! They're strange, the two of them, but they are kind, I promise. If you ever meet them, please don't run. You're braver than you think.
— Amber

Handout 2 — Tighnari's Field Notes on the Withering (cue: start of Scene 3)

  • Withering Zones radiate from a "Tumor" — a pulsing black growth.
  • Non-elemental beings entering the Zone suffer lethargy, then hallucinations, then collapse.
  • Fungi and Whopperflowers grow faster and meaner inside Zones.
  • The Zone shrinks when the Tumor is destroyed. Elemental energy burns it.
  • Reports of Zones have tripled this year. I do not know why. Keep this note close.

Handout 3 — The Six Vision Cards (cue: Scene 4 puzzle)

Print six small index cards. On each, put one of: WHITE TREE, RED SKY, FORGOTTEN NAME, BLIND BIRD, BURNING ROOT, EMPTY THRONE. See the Scene 4 puzzle for setup.

Handout 4 — Sealed "Whispered Memory" Envelope (cue: Scene 4, on solve)

Inside a sealed envelope write:
"...World... Forget me... I am the root that burned for you, and the name you are forbidden to speak. If you would find me, find the one who has not yet died."
Seal it. Players open it only when the puzzle is solved.

Handout 5 — Akasha Terminal Card (cue: start of Scene 6)

AKASHA TERMINAL — Visitor Pass
Issued by the Sumeru Akademiya. Ask, and the leaves shall answer. Restrictions: The Akasha cannot speak of the Archon. The Akasha cannot speak of Irminsul. The Akasha cannot speak of itself.

Handout 6 — Alhaitham's Terms (cue: mid-Scene 7)

I will tell you what the Akademiya lost only after you have done one thing: become a customer of the merchant Dori. Do not haggle. Do not ask what her wares are used for. Return to me when the deal is done.
— A.

Scene 1 — Out of The Chasm, Into the Green

0:00-0:20 RP LORE

The tunnel spits you out into light so green it feels wet. Vines the thickness of your arm drape the rocks behind you; the air is a living thing, heavy with pollen and the calls of birds you have no names for. Somewhere distant, a waterfall hisses. The Chasm's dust is still on your boots, but already the forest is trying to claim it — a pale-green lichen is climbing the hem of your cloak while you stand there.

Paimon goes silent for three whole seconds. Then she says, very quietly, "Paimon... Paimon didn't know a place could breathe."

A woman in Akademiya robes is walking straight toward you along the path, muttering to herself, eyes fixed on nothing. She is going to walk directly through your group without noticing.

DM Beats

  • Let players react to the sensory overload — ask each one what their character does first (touch a leaf, taste the air, check the sky).
  • Haypasia will not respond to anything. She mutters fragments: "...the root... the root remembers..." Players can try to block her, follow her, grab her — she just walks around them like sleepwalking.
  • Paimon suggests following her on the logic that she must be going somewhere with people.
  • If players try Insight/Perception: Haypasia's pupils are unfocused, she smells faintly of bitter incense, her hands are trembling.

PLAYER CHOICE — How to handle Haypasia

Setup: Haypasia won't respond. The party must decide how to engage.

  • Option A: Follow her silently → leads straight to the cave and the vision in Scene 4. Standard path.
  • Option B: Physically restrain her → she screams, a Forest Ranger patrol (Collei and one ally) arrives within minutes, skipping the Scene 2 "stumble into Gandharva Ville" intro. Collei is suspicious of the party from the start; RP in Scenes 2-3 is frostier.
  • Option C: Let her go and explore the forest alone → triggers Combat 1 (Forest Ambush) earlier than planned. They later find her cave in Scene 4 via Tighnari.

Persistent impact: Choice B permanently changes Collei's opening attitude; choice C means the party owes Tighnari a favour when he rescues them, which he will call in during Scene 5.

Scene 2 — Gandharva Ville

0:20-0:50 RP

You wake on a woven mat under a canopy of broad leaves. The ceiling is alive — literally — a trellis of flowering vines that someone has trained into the shape of a roof. A fox-eared young man is grinding something in a stone bowl with the violence of a man who has had a long day. Beside him, a quieter girl with mint-green hair is watching you with the careful, measured eyes of someone who has learned not to get too close to anything.

The fox-eared man looks up, sees you're awake, and says flatly: "You were unconscious in Haypasia's cave. I am Tighnari, Forest Watcher. This is Collei. You are not in danger. You are, however, in our way. So please — tell me exactly what you think you saw."

DM Beats

  • Tighnari is testing them. He lies by omission: tells them the vision is "a known hallucinogenic effect of cave incense" and downplays Irminsul. Let Insight catch him at it (DC 14).
  • Collei hangs back. She is nervous around strangers; if a player approaches her gently, she lights up when the topic of Mondstadt comes up.
  • Hand Collei's letter from Amber (Handout 1) to whichever player seems to bond with her. This is a persistent trust token.
  • Paimon reaches out to pat Collei. Collei flinches hard and retreats. Do not explain it yet.
  • Tighnari offers the party lodging for the night and breakfast in the morning, in exchange for accompanying Collei on her "easy" patrol.

Scene 3 — Collei's Patrol and the Withering Zone

0:50-1:25 COMBAT LORE

Hand out Handout 2 — Tighnari's Field Notes on the Withering as Collei briefs the party before setting out.

The path Collei leads you down is barely a path at all — just where her boots have pressed the ferns flat on previous patrols. She points at things as she walks: a fruit that's safe to eat, a fruit that will kill you in four hours, a bird whose call means rain. Then she stops, mid-sentence. The birdsong has stopped too.

Ahead, the forest has gone wrong. A patch maybe thirty paces across where the leaves have curled black, where the air shimmers the way heat shimmers off a forge, and where a single pulsing lump the size of a curled dog sits at the centre, breathing slow and wet. Collei whispers: "A Tumor. We shouldn't be here. I shouldn't be here. But you — you have elements. You can burn it out. Please."

Encounter — Cleansing the Withering Tumor

Setup: The party must destroy the pulsing Tumor at the heart of the Withering Zone. Fungi corrupted by the Zone rise to defend it as soon as the party steps within ten paces of the centre.

Enemies: 1× grounded_earth_shroom [Rare], 2× floating_nature_fungus [Common], 2× nature_samachurl [Uncommon]. The Tumor itself is a stationary objective (treat as 40 HP, no defenses, only takes damage from elemental attacks).

Goal: Destroy the Tumor. Enemies respawn on a 3-round timer until the Tumor dies, so the party must prioritize.

Twist: Any non-elemental party member who ends a turn inside the Zone takes 1d6 necrotic and gains "Withered" (disadvantage on next roll). Collei refuses to step in; she can shoot arrows from the edge but tires fast.

Reward: The Zone shrivels to nothing, the birdsong returns, and Tighnari (arriving at the tail end) is visibly relieved. This is the moment his lies stop.

DM Beats After Combat

  • Tighnari arrives as the Tumor dies. He watches for a long moment, then says: "The incense in that cave did not hallucinate you. I owe you the truth."
  • Lore dump callout (below) — do not monologue, bullet it out across the walk back.
  • A small Dusk Bird lands on Tighnari's shoulder carrying a scrap of leaf-paper. Haypasia is awake, starving, and begging for food and water.

Lore Dump — Tighnari Breaks His Rule

  • There were two Dendro Archons. Greater Lord Rukkhadevata died in the Cataclysm 500 years ago.
  • Lesser Lord Kusanali inherited the throne but no one sees her. The Sages hold real power now.
  • Irminsul is a tree at the bottom of the world. Its roots are the Ley Lines. It remembers everything that has ever happened.
  • The Withering is Irminsul getting sick. No one knows why, and no one knows how to cure a god-tree.
  • Your vision was not a hallucination. You touched Irminsul. Haypasia trained for three years to do that on purpose. You did it by accident, once, and lived.

Scene 4 — Haypasia's Cave and the Vision Puzzle

1:25-2:00 PUZZLE LORE

The cave smells of cold stone and the faint, sticky-sweet residue of burnt incense. Haypasia is awake this time — propped against the wall, hollow-cheeked, eyes bright with the kind of joy that only shows up in people who have been too close to something they shouldn't have touched. "You came back," she says. "And you saw it. I can tell. You have the look." She gestures weakly at a stone altar carved with six shallow depressions, and a small wooden box beside it. "The Aranara left this. They said one of you would need it. Do you want to remember what you saw? Properly?"

The Aranara's Memory Altar

What the DM prepares: Six small index cards, each labelled with one of: WHITE TREE, RED SKY, FORGOTTEN NAME, BLIND BIRD, BURNING ROOT, EMPTY THRONE. Also: one sealed envelope labelled "Whispered Memory" containing Handout 4.

The setup on the table: Lay the six cards face-up in a row between the players. Describe six shallow stone depressions on the altar arranged in a circle. The players must place three cards into the circle, in order, to form the memory the Traveler saw.

The clue (read this aloud):

"Three I saw, and three alone:
First the place where the god had gone,
Then the sky that would not hold,
Last the tree that burned for me."

The solution: EMPTY THRONE → RED SKY → BURNING ROOT. (The "place where the god had gone" is the throne; "sky that would not hold" is red sky; "tree that burned for me" is the burning root — not "white tree", which is a decoy referring to Irminsul's present state, not the memory.)

What unlocks: When the correct three cards are placed in order, Haypasia gasps and hands over the sealed "Whispered Memory" envelope. Inside (Handout 4) are the actual words Greater Lord Rukkhadevata spoke: "...World... Forget me... I am the root that burned for you, and the name you are forbidden to speak. If you would find me, find the one who has not yet died." This foreshadows Scene 8 (Mizri speaks the same words) AND the next session's Kusanali meeting.

Hints (escalating):

  1. "The poem speaks of three things, not six. Two of these cards are lures." (Points them at WHITE TREE and FORGOTTEN NAME and BLIND BIRD being decoys; the correct three emerge by elimination.)
  2. "Haypasia murmurs: 'The throne is always first. The sky second. The root comes last because the root is where it ends.'"
  3. Direct: "Haypasia reaches out, trembling, and points: empty throne, red sky, burning root."

PLAYER CHOICE — Share the vision, or keep it secret?

Setup: After opening the envelope, do the players tell Tighnari and Haypasia what the words say?

  • Option A: Share with Tighnari → He commits fully to helping the party reach Kusanali and writes them a letter of introduction to Rohawi. Scene 6 opens easier.
  • Option B: Share only with Haypasia → She whispers that the Aranara can lead the party to Kusanali by dream. Unlocks an optional "Aranara Dream Path" branch in the next session.
  • Option C: Tell no one → Keeps the secret safe but Tighnari's introduction to Rohawi in Sumeru City is lukewarm; Scene 6 is harder and Rohawi will not speak freely.

Persistent impact: This choice decides whether the party has human, dream, or no allies coming into Sumeru City next session.

Scene 5 — The Journey to Sumeru City

2:00-2:25 COMBAT RP

The road out of the rainforest climbs. For the first time in two days, you see the sun directly — Sumeru City sits on the horizon like a great pale mushroom cap, terraced gardens cascading down its slopes, and even from this distance you can hear the Akademiya bells. Before you can admire it for long, the undergrowth on your left rustles wrongly.

Encounter — Hilichurl Ambush on the Ridge

Setup: A band of hilichurls have been displaced by the Withering and are attacking anything that moves on the road.

Enemies: 1× wooden_shield_mitachurl [Rare], 3× hillichurl_fighter [Common], 1× nature_samachurl [Uncommon]

Goal: Clear the road. The mitachurl guards a small cart with a dead merchant and Scene 6 hooks inside (a sealed letter addressed to "Asfand, Corps of Thirty, Sumeru City").

Twist: The samachurl will try to summon a vine pillar on round 2, creating difficult terrain across the middle of the battlefield. Destroying it (10 HP) takes it out.

Reward: The letter (a real NPC introduction to Asfand, bypassing a Scene 6 roadblock), 200 mora, a minor healing item.

Scene 6 — Sumeru City Arrival and the Akasha

2:25-2:50 RP LORE

At the city gate, hand out Handout 5 — Akasha Terminal Card, one per player.

The guard at the gate presses a small circular device into each of your palms. "New policy," he says without looking up. "The Akasha. Ask, and the leaves shall answer. Mandatory for visitors." The device warms against your skin and a soft, toneless voice seems to speak inside your ear, just behind the thought. "Welcome to Sumeru City. What do you wish to know?"

DM Beats

  • Let every player ask the Akasha one question. The Akasha refuses to discuss: the Archon, Irminsul, itself, and anything "classified" — use these refusals to build dread.
  • Rohawi (Tighnari's contact) is found in a small library alcove. His warmth depends on the Scene 4 choice.
  • Katheryne at the Adventurers' Guild speaks strangely — too informally, too aware. Flag this for later campaigns.
  • Dunyarzad, a pale young woman in fine robes, approaches them unprompted. "Excuse me — are you the travelers from Mondstadt?" Dehya, her bodyguard, looms two paces behind, arms crossed.

PLAYER CHOICE — Dunyarzad's Festival

Setup: Dunyarzad wants to revive the Sabzeruz Festival. She believes Kusanali will appear if her birthday is celebrated. She asks for help.

  • Option A: Agree, help her plan the festival → unlocks the festival subplot for next session; Dehya becomes a recurring ally; Nilou performs for them for free.
  • Option B: Politely defer ("we'll help if we can, but we're chasing a lead first") → Dunyarzad still gives them a letter of introduction to people in Port Ormos. The festival plot remains available.
  • Option C: Refuse — "we don't have time" → Dunyarzad is hurt, Dehya takes offense. The party loses access to Nilou's social network, and Scene 7 starts with Alhaitham already annoyed because Dehya has warned him about them.

Persistent impact: The festival is next session's climax. This choice decides whether the party has planners, a reluctant invitation, or is an outside observer.

Scene 7 — Port Ormos and Alhaitham

2:50-3:15 RP

Port Ormos hits you like a wall of noise. Gulls, shouting merchants, the wet slap of nets on stone, the salt-and-cinnamon smell of the spice wharves. Somewhere in the middle of it all, a fight is about to start: on one side, three scarred men in red desert robes; on the other, a tall, grey-haired man in Akademiya colours who is reading a book. Reading. Actually reading, as the three men shout at him.

He turns a page. He says, without looking up: "If you are going to threaten me, at least make it interesting. I have read this paragraph four times and it is still more compelling than you."

DM Beats

  • The Eremites back off with a promise of a fight "in three days at the old shrine." Alhaitham closes his book and sees the party watching.
  • Alhaitham is willing to share info on the stolen Akademiya item (a Knowledge Capsule) but ONLY after the party completes one task for him.
  • Hand them Handout 6 — Alhaitham's Terms.
  • The task: buy something from Dori, a merchant the Akademiya has blacklisted. They must not haggle. They must not ask what it's for. Pay with a pouch of mora he hands them.
  • Dori is a fun RP moment — she's thrilled, she overcharges shamelessly, she hands them a small, heavy, sealed package.

PLAYER CHOICE — Dori's Package

Setup: The package Dori hands over is warm, vibrating faintly. Alhaitham told them not to open it. Do they peek?

  • Option A: Deliver it unopened → Alhaitham gives them the full briefing on the Capsule and comes to Scene 8 as a full ally. Standard path.
  • Option B: Peek inside → It contains a disabled Akasha Terminal jailbroken to intercept Akademiya signals. Alhaitham notices the tamper-seal, gives a shorter briefing, and trusts the party less in future sessions.
  • Option C: Keep it / double-cross Alhaitham → They now have a jailbroken Akasha Terminal (powerful item) but Alhaitham walks away, and Scene 8 happens WITHOUT him — the party must stop Mizri alone. Add a second Eremite wave in that fight.

Persistent impact: This decides Alhaitham's long-term relationship with the party and whether Scene 8 is easier or much harder.

Scene 8 — The Shrine Fight and the Broken Mind

3:15-3:45 COMBAT

The old shrine sits in a bowl of cracked flagstones, half-buried in vines. The Eremites are already there — Mizri in the centre, holding something small and metallic that pulses with a sick, green light. Alhaitham draws his sword without ceremony. "Keep him talking," he says quietly. "I need thirty seconds at the Terminal on his wrist, and this ends without anyone dying. Probably."

Mizri raises the thing he's holding to his forehead. His eyes roll back. When he speaks, it is not his voice. It is layered, older, and it says: "...World... Forget me..."

Encounter — Mizri and the Knowledge Capsule

Setup: Climactic set-piece. Mizri is not the party's target — reaching Mizri is. Alhaitham needs thirty seconds (three rounds) adjacent to Mizri to sever the Akasha connection. The party must get him there and keep him alive.

Enemies: 1× eremite_ravenbeak_halberdier [Rare] (Mizri, boss — but unattackable until the Capsule is severed), 2× eremite_linebreaker [Uncommon], 2× eremite_sword_dancer [Uncommon], 1× electric_abyss_mage [Rare] (arrives on round 2 as Mizri's connection attracts it like a moth)

If the party double-crossed Alhaitham (Scene 7, Option C): Alhaitham is absent. Add 2× eremite_sword_dancer [Uncommon] and the party must destroy the Capsule themselves by attacking Mizri directly (Boss AC). Much harder.

Goal: Get Alhaitham adjacent to Mizri for 3 rounds, OR destroy the Capsule, before Mizri's mind shatters entirely (soft fail timer: end of round 5).

Twist: On round 3, Mizri's connection to Irminsul causes a wave of dead-memory fog to pulse out from him — anyone within 15 ft must save or be stunned one round and see a fragment of someone else's memory (DM: describe something from the party's own pasts they thought was forgotten). This is a story beat as much as a mechanic.

Reward: Mizri is taken alive by arriving Matra (secret police). Alhaitham pockets the Capsule without comment. The party overhears Mizri's final words: "...World... Forget me..." — exactly the words from the vision.

DM Beats After Combat

  • The party and Alhaitham share information — but let both sides hold something back. Alhaitham does NOT mention pocketing the Capsule. Players may choose to mention their vision or not.
  • Alhaitham compares Mizri's symptoms to Akademiya researchers who have gone insane trying to access Irminsul directly. "The Capsule does not grant divine knowledge. It breaks the channel through which you seek it."
  • The sun is setting over Port Ormos. Dunyarzad's Sabzeruz Festival is tomorrow.

Scene 9 — Return to Sumeru City and the Festival Eve

3:45-4:00 RP

You walk back toward Sumeru City in the long red light of evening. The Akasha Terminal in your palm is quiet now. Somewhere ahead, lanterns are being hung in the Bazaar — strings and strings of them, every one painted with a small purple flower you do not recognize. You can hear music drifting down the road: a flute, a small drum, and a woman's voice practicing a phrase, stopping, and trying it again.

Paimon looks at you and says, for once, very quietly: "You know Paimon heard what Mizri said. That was your vision. Word for word. What is that voice?"

The lanterns ahead of you flicker in a sudden small wind. The flower on every one of them — the Padisarah — is, just for a single moment, a deeper, truer purple than any dye could make.
And then it's gone.

DM Beats — Session Close

  • Give each player one sentence of free RP — what their character is thinking or doing as the session closes.
  • Tease: the Sabzeruz Festival is tomorrow. Dunyarzad is waiting.
  • End on Paimon's question. Do NOT answer it.

If You Run Short

  • Cut Scene 5 combat — narrate the hilichurl ambush as "a brief scuffle" and give them the letter directly. Saves ~20 min.
  • Shorten Scene 6 — skip the Akasha exploration and have Dunyarzad approach them on the street within two minutes. Saves ~10 min.
  • Cut the Dori errand from Scene 7 — have Alhaitham just tell them the information for a favour later. Saves ~15 min.
  • Protect at all costs: Scene 4 (vision puzzle), Scene 3 (Withering Zone cleanse), Scene 8 (Shrine fight). These are load-bearing.

If You Have Extra Time

  • Add Nilou's dance audition — Dunyarzad takes them to meet Nilou, who performs a short preview of the Dance of Sabzeruz. Push players to ask about the Goddess of Flowers and the Padisarah.
  • Add a Katheryne scene — let players notice her strange speech and pursue it. Plant seeds for Fontaine reveals later.
  • Add a second Withering Zone — on the road to Sumeru City, reuse the Scene 3 encounter with different fungi (whirling_fire_fungus, stretchy_wind_fungus).
  • Add an Aranara encounter — a small creature only one player can see, delivering a cryptic song. Seeds next session's dream subplot.
  • Expand the Dori negotiation — let her try to sell them three weird items and force them to pick one blind.

Lead-In to Next Session

The session closes on the evening before the Sabzeruz Festival. Next session opens at sunrise in the Bazaar as Dunyarzad greets the party and Nilou prepares for her dance.

Cliffhanger: Paimon's question ("What is that voice?") hangs unanswered. The Padisarah flowers flickered real for a heartbeat. Someone is listening.

DM prep for next session:

  • Decide how the Sabzeruz Festival plays out based on the Scene 6 choice (planner / deferred / refused).
  • If players chose the Aranara Dream Path in Scene 4, prepare a dream sequence as the session's cold open.
  • Alhaitham's retained Capsule will resurface. Decide whether he tells the party before they find out another way.
  • Lesser Lord Kusanali is the target of the next session's climax. Review her as an NPC — small, strange, kind, painfully earnest.
  • Pre-load enemies for the Sabzeruz disruption encounter: suggest eremite_floral_ring_dancer, eremite_sunfrost, and a surprise electric_abyss_mage.
