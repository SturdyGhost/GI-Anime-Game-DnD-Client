1. Combat

Combat is turn-based. Each player takes their turn in a set order, performing movement, attacks, and item usage. Enemies act between player turns depending on their type.

☰ Turn Order

Turn order is set during Battle Prep before combat begins. Players decide the order they will act in. This order stays fixed for the entire combat encounter but can be rearranged between encounters.

Boss Turn Timing
World Bosses act after every other player action.
Quest Bosses act after every player action.

🚶 Movement

Each player can move a number of tiles per turn. Movement generally happens before attacking unless the ability specifies otherwise.

Distance Calculation
Tile distance is calculated using 4 cardinal directions only (up/down/left/right), not diagonals. This applies to movement, ability range, and all distance checks.

⚔ Attacking & Defending

Roll Resolution

When a player attacks, both sides roll dice simultaneously:

  • Attacker rolls their attack die (determined by their Attack stat)
  • Defender rolls their defense die (determined by their Defense stat)
  • If the attacker's roll is higher, the attack hits
  • The difference between the rolls determines the damage die: round down to the nearest standard die size
  • The attacker then rolls that damage die to determine base damage before any multipliers or bonuses
Example
Attacker rolls 15, Defender rolls 8. Difference = 7.
Rounded down to nearest die: D6. Attacker rolls a D6 for base damage.

Critical Hits

A critical hit occurs when the attacker's roll exceeds 20 (by default). Weapons and effects can raise or lower this threshold. Critical hits apply bonus damage and trigger any ON_CRIT effects from weapons, artifacts, or abilities.

Multi-Hit Diminishing Returns

Successive hits on the same target from a single attack deal diminishing damage unless the ability specifies otherwise:

Hit #DamageFraction
1st Hit100%Full damage
2nd Hit33%1/3 damage
3rd Hit11%1/9 damage
4th Hit3.6%1/27 damage
5th+ Hit<2%Continues at 1/3 per hit
Exceptions
Some abilities explicitly bypass diminishing returns. The more situational or difficult an ability's AoE is to land, the less likely this rule applies. Abilities will state if they are an exception.

🍴 Items in Combat

Using an item does not consume your action for the turn. You may attack and use an item in the same turn. However, you may only use one item per turn.

  • Multiple heal-over-time foods do not stack. Using a new one replaces the active one.
  • You can use an instant heal food while keeping an active heal-over-time effect.
  • Soups are used to revive downed players/companions.

🛡 Shields

Shields are directional and rotate with your character. By default a shield covers one of 4 cardinal directions unless the ability specifies multiple or all directions.

  • Shields absorb damage before HP when hit from the shielded direction
  • Default shield duration is 4 turns unless specified otherwise
  • When a shield's health reaches 0 (broken by damage), the unit receives Shield-Break status for 2 turns
  • When a shield expires naturally (turns run out), no penalty is applied
  • You control when your shield blocks — you can choose to save it for a specific attack
Shield-Break
Incoming damage is doubled. Cannot gain a new shield or eat food items while active. Lasts 2 turns.

⚡ Burst Charges

Burst charges are the resource used to activate your Burst ability. They are generated primarily through Skills and Charged Attacks.

  • After using a Skill or Charged Attack, the ability typically has you roll a D4 for burst charges gained
  • The roll result is then multiplied by your Energy Recharge stat (rounded down, minimum 1)
  • Once you reach your Burst's charge cost, you can use it — charges are consumed on use

2. Stats & Dice

Your stats determine the dice you roll in combat. Higher stats mean bigger dice, and bigger dice mean better outcomes.

⚙ Stat Allocation

Each player starts with 45 base stat points they can allocate freely. The cost per point varies by stat:

StatCost per PointGain per Point
Health1 point+2 HP
Attack1 point+1 ATK
Defense1 point+1 DEF
Elemental Mastery1 point+1 EM
Energy Recharge1 point+0.1 ER
Critical Damage1 point+0.1 CD

Upon ascending, stats reset to base values. Each ascension level grants that level's worth of bonus points (e.g., ascending to rank 3 gives 3 additional points).

Respec
Outside of battle, you can redistribute your base stat points by making an offering to Paimon. If accepted, you can reallocate freely.

🎲 Dice Scaling

Your stat value determines what die you roll. Values round down to the nearest valid die size:

The D20 Threshold
You must reach exactly 20 to qualify for a D20. A stat of 19 still rounds down to D12. This encourages balanced stat distribution rather than maxing a single stat.

📊 Stat Descriptions

Health (HP)

Your hit points. When HP reaches 0, you are downed and must be revived with a soup item. Cost: 2 HP per stat point allocated. Also boosted by weapon effects, artifact stats, and food buffs.

Attack (ATK)

Determines your attack die size. Higher attack means you roll bigger dice when attacking, making it more likely to beat the defender's roll and deal more damage.

Defense (DEF)

Determines your defense die size. When attacked, you roll your defense die — the higher you roll, the less damage you take (or block the attack entirely).

Elemental Mastery (EM)

Affects the strength of elemental reactions and certain ability calculations. Your EM stat determines the die used for reaction-related rolls.

Energy Recharge (ER)

Multiplies burst charge gain. When you gain burst charges (e.g., rolling a D4 after a skill), the result is multiplied by your ER value, rounded down (minimum 1). Higher ER means faster burst access.

Critical Damage (CD)

Bonus damage multiplier applied on critical hits. Added on top of the base damage when your attack roll exceeds the crit threshold.

3. Elements & Reactions

Seven elements govern combat. Applying one element to a target that already has a different element triggers a reaction — some helpful, some disastrous.

🔥 Fire (Pyro) 💧 Water (Hydro) ⚡ Electric (Electro) ❄ Ice (Cryo) 💨 Wind (Anemo) ⚖ Earth (Geo) 🌱 Nature (Dendro)

💥 How Reactions Work

When an attack applies an element to a target that already has a different element applied, a reaction triggers. The reaction depends on which element was already on the target (first) and which element is being applied (second). Order matters for almost every reaction — the same two elements can produce very different outcomes depending on which was applied first.

Favorable vs Unfavorable
FAVORABLE — Benefits the attacker
UNFAVORABLE — Hurts the attacker or helps the defender
NEUTRAL — Affects both sides equally

Reaction damage is separate from the attack's own damage and is not affected by multipliers (like crits) unless specified.

4. Status Effects

Status effects alter a unit's capabilities for a set duration. Multiple statuses can be active simultaneously. Some cancel out (e.g., Slow + Quick = normal speed).

🚫 Control Effects

Stun Your turn is skipped entirely. Cannot act, move, or use any abilities — including "at any time" abilities.
Root Cannot move. Abilities with built-in movement (blinks, dashes) cannot be used. You can still attack.
Blind Your target is randomized by dice roll. AoE abilities center on the random target. Friendly fire is enabled.
Disarm Weapon is knocked away. Cannot attack until recovered. May swap to same-type weapon from inventory (counts as action). Weapon lost if not picked up by battle end.
Taunt Forces enemies to target the taunting unit.
Fear Forces enemies to move away from and target the ally furthest from the fearing unit.

🚶 Movement Effects

Slow Each tile of movement counts as 2. (Move half as far.)
Quick Each tile of movement counts as 0.5. (Move twice as far.)
Hot Feet Must use full movement before attacking. Cannot end on your starting tile.
Slippery Next movement slides your full distance in the chosen direction. Cannot stop early.
Pinned Cannot rotate or change facing direction.
Loner If any entity is on surrounding tiles at end of turn, your next attack deals no damage.

⚔ Combat Effects

Advantage Roll twice, take the better result.
Disadvantage Roll twice, take the worse result.
Camouflage Cannot be seen or targeted by enemies. Ends when you attack or use an ability on an ally.
Dazed Must act, but can only use Basic Attacks. Skills and Bursts are locked.
Collateral Damage When you deal damage, 25% of the final damage is also dealt to the ally closest to you.

🔴 Debuffs

Burst Bust Cannot generate burst charges while active.
Skill Suck Skill cooldowns do not decrease while active.
Unlucky Overrides your daily luck. Bad things happen.
Overheated Using an ability with a cooldown increases its cooldown by 1 additional turn.
Locked In Must repeat the same actions from last turn in the same order, if possible.
Shield-Break Incoming damage is doubled. Cannot gain a new shield or eat food items. Lasts 2 turns.

🟢 Buffs

Lucky Overrides your daily luck. Good things happen.
En Garde The next attack against this unit deals no damage and the attacker is stunned for 1 turn.
Reflect The next attack is reflected back at the attacker.

5. Abilities & Kits

Every character has a set of abilities (their "kit") tied to their element and weapon type. Abilities are modular — there's no fixed template.
