🌿 Nature Kit Reference

Passives, Talents, and Constellations for Brian C., Brian F., and Dylan

Brian C.

Claymore Earth / Nature
Abilities
Basic Attack
Element: Physical Dice: 1d10 Range: 5 tiles Movement: 4 tiles
Slash all adjacent enemies. After attacking, move up to 4 tiles.
Charged Attack
Element: Nature Dice: 1d10 AoE: Global (adjacent) Movement: 1 tile
Slash all adjacent enemies. Applies Nature. Gain 1d4 burst charges × ER. Move 1 tile before or after.
  • Applies Nature to target
  • Gain 1d4 burst charges × Energy Recharge
Elemental Skill
Element: Nature Dice: 1d10 AoE: Global Movement: 1 tile Cooldown: 2 turns
Choose a tile within 2 tiles. Enemies on that tile and adjacent tiles take Nature damage.

Escalation: d4 → d6 → d8 → d10 → d12 → d20 (must roll above half to succeed). Damage = cumulative result of success rolls (pre-multiplier). Failure results in 0 damage. Gain 1d4 burst charges × ER after.
  • Gain 1d4 burst charges × Energy Recharge after
Elemental Burst
Element: Physical Dice: 1d10 Cost: 12 charges Movement: 0
Cannot move this turn. Perform up to 2 Basic or Charged Attacks as Nature using EM. Double Nature reactions triggered. Roll 1d4; on 4 regain 6 charges.
  • Perform up to 2 Basic or Charged Attacks
  • Double Nature reactions triggered
  • Cannot move this turn
Passive
Nature Passive
Before an escalation roll, lose 2 HP to reduce the requirement by 1 (multiple times allowed). The requirement cannot be reduced below 1.
Talents
Push Further – First escalation each turn: −1 requirement. ✓ Selected
Nature Takes Its Due – If 10+ HP lost this turn, next Nature damage doubled. ✓ Selected
Lucky Collapse – On failure, deal half highest successful roll.
High Roller – Each voluntary escalation grants +1 burst charge.
Nature Remembers – 10+ total damage grants +2 burst charges.
Each time you go out to gather materials, gain the ability to also collect 1 D6 of any resource/material of your choice (elemental gems are not counted as part of this).
Learn how to use another weapon of your choice, doing so will allow you to equip either weapon type. Note: your basic and charged attacks will change based on the type of weapon being used.
Constellations
Weak
Add 1 D4 to your base defense ✓ Selected
Once per battle, swap spaces with an ally. ✓ Selected
When gathering materials, you may choose which material cache you go to rather than rolling for it. ✓ Selected

Medium

Allow yourself to choose another talent with that talent filling this constellation spot for each kit.
On defense rolls gain the ability to roll twice and choose the better of the two rolls.

On your normal attack gain the option to have it deal either physical or elemental damage regardless of your weapon.
Strong
Gain the ability to have 2 food Buffs Active at once.
On your charged attack gain the option to have it deal either physical or elemental damage regardless of your weapon.
Whatever movement you have for your basic attack, enable it for your charged attack and elemental burst.
Add 1.0 to your base Energy Recharge.
Add 1.0 to your base Crit Damage.
Once Per Battle guarantees the next attack will be a D20.
Once Per Session, if your health drops to 0 and you are downed, pump yourself up, and restore yourself up to half health without consuming any items.
You can no longer hurt your allies with any of your attacks.

Dylan

Catalyst Nature
Abilities
Basic Attack
Element: Nature Dice: 1d20 Range: 3 tiles Movement: 4 tiles
Attack within 3 tiles. Pull small units 1 tile on hit. Move up to 4 tiles after.
Charged Attack
Element: Nature Dice: 1d20 Range: 2 tiles Movement: 1 tile
Attack any unit within 2 tiles. Pull small units 1 tile. Any hit are Rooted until end of next turn. Gain 1d4 burst charges × ER. Move 1 tile before or after.
  • Rooted until end of next turn
  • Gain 1d4 burst charges × Energy Recharge
Elemental Skill
Element: Nature Dice: 1d20 Range: 5 tiles Radius: 2 tiles Movement: 1 tile Cooldown: 3 turns
Choose tile within 5 tiles. 2-tile radius eruption. Small enemies pulled to center. All enemies take Nature damage and are Rooted.
  • All enemies are Rooted
  • Apply Nature element
Elemental Burst – Wild Shape
Element: Nature Dice: 1d20 (EM die) Cost: 14 charges Duration: 2 turns Movement: 0
Roll EM die for transformation (2 turns). Cannot move on activation turn.
Roll 1–4: Turn into a heal. A party member may interact with it to heal 1d6+EM. If no one interacts by end of turn, it automatically heals the closest ally for 1d4+EM instead.
Roll 5–14: +2 movement, pull on attacks, Charged attacks root, temp HP = 1/4 EM.
Roll 15–19: All attacks and abilities gain +1 tile to their area of effect. At the end of your turn, all enemies adjacent to you become Rooted. Temp HP = 1/2 EM.
Roll 20+: Choose form.
  • Cannot move on activation turn
