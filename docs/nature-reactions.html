🌿 Nature Elemental Reactions

Nature ↔ Electric and Nature ↔ Ice — effect descriptions and element order

🌿 Nature ⟷ ⚡ Electric
Nature applied → Electric target
✓ Favorable
Trigger: A Nature attack hits an enemy that already has the Electric element applied.
Fire a piercing vine bolt at any target within 4 tiles dealing D8 Nature damage to the target.
  • Bonus ranged attack — 4-tile range vine bolt
  • D8 Nature damage to the selected target
  • Piercing — hits regardless of positioning
Electric applied → Nature target
✗ Unfavorable
Trigger: An Electric attack hits an enemy that already has the Nature element applied.
The vine reflects back at the attacker dealing D6 Nature damage and rooting them for 1 turn.
  • Reflect damage — D6 Nature damage back to the attacker
  • Root — attacker cannot move for 1 turn

⚠ Punishes the attacker — be careful applying Electric to Nature-marked enemies!

🌿 Nature ⟷ ❄ Ice
Nature applied → Ice target
✓ Favorable
Trigger: A Nature attack hits an enemy that already has the Ice element applied.
The target deals −2 damage for 2 turns. Earth attacks against them deal +6 damage for 1 turn.
  • −2 damage debuff on the target for 2 turns
  • Earth vulnerability — Earth attacks deal +6 bonus damage to the target for 1 turn
  • Great setup for Earth-element follow-ups
Ice applied → Nature target
✗ Unfavorable
Trigger: An Ice attack hits an enemy that already has the Nature element applied.
The attacker is encased in ice, stunned for 1 turn. They additionally lose the ability to apply any element for the rest of the battle.
  • Stun — attacker skips their next turn
  • Permanent element lock — attacker can no longer apply any element for the rest of the battle

⚠ One of the harshest penalties in the game — completely shuts down elemental play for the attacker!
